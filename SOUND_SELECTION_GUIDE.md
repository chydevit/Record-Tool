## 🔍 Quick Comparison

### Similar Sounds - Choose One

**Default vs Soft**
- Default: More presence, professional
- Soft: More subtle, gentle

**Mechanical vs Tick**
- Mechanical: Fuller, keyboard-like
- Tick: Minimal, precise

**Pop vs Bubble**
- Pop: Wider sweep, bouncy
- Bubble: Wobble effect, playful

**Glass vs Bell**
- Glass: Higher pitch, longer ring
- Bell: Harmonic, musical

**Drum vs Thud**
- Drum: Punchy, rhythmic
- Thud: Heavier, deeper

**Laser vs Zap**
- Laser: Smoother sweep, sci-fi
- Zap: Sharper, electric

**Snap vs Tick**
- Snap: Percussive, fuller
- Tick: Minimal, precise

**Whomp vs Thud**
- Whomp: Filtered, bass drop
- Thud: Direct, impact

## 🚀 Quick Start

**Never used click sounds before?**
Start with: **Default** (safe, professional)

**Want something different?**
Try: **Pop** (fun), **Swoosh** (modern), or **Bell** (elegant)

**Making gaming content?**
Go with: **Mechanical** or **Coin**

**Tutorial or education?**
Choose: **Soft** or **Wooden**

**Want to stand out?**
Experiment with: **Laser**, **Zap**, or **Whomp**

---

**Remember**: You can always change the sound mid-project! Test different sounds to find what fits your content best.
