## Settings Location

1. Open Video Editor
2. Click **Cursor Settings** (cursor icon)
3. Find **CLICK SOUNDS** section
4. Toggle ON
5. Select from **Sound Style** dropdown
6. Adjust **Volume** slider (0-100%)

## Default Settings

- **Enabled**: Yes
- **Volume**: 30%
- **Variant**: Default

## Tips

💡 **Start with Default** if unsure  
💡 **Use Soft** for long videos  
💡 **Try Pop** for fun content  
💡 **Use Tick** for precision  
💡 **Adjust volume** to taste  

## Troubleshooting

**No sound?**
- Check toggle is ON
- Check volume > 0%
- Check browser audio not muted

**Too loud?**
- Reduce volume slider
- Try Soft or Tick

**Too quiet?**
- Increase volume slider
- Try Snap or Drum
